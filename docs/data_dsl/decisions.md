# Integrated DSL decisions

1. The four active labels are Full Stack Developer, Machine Learning Engineer,
   Cybersecurity Specialist and Data Architect. Preserve every satisfied label.
2. Require 1..59 experience years and 1..5 qualification levels in current exports.
   These are published team bounds. The historical standalone model is retained
   separately, with its wider 0..60 range and extra Software Architect label.
3. Technologies and competence areas have separate namespaces. Each area level
   must be explicitly stated; mentioning a technology does not imply proficiency.
4. Preserve PostgreSQL/MySQL evidence in the DSL; map to the contributor's SQL
   symbol only for classifier compatibility. Data models consume canonically
   ordered category tokens, as allowed by the assignment.
5. Retain the contributor modules and their source hashes. Adapters live in our
   own modules. Syntax, business validation and automaton acceptance remain
   separate responsibilities.
6. Keep historical examples intact and provide current examples in a distinct
   directory. No sample with Software Architect proves current four-profile export.
